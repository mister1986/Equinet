2. Data-based Meta Ads plan

The setup uses only the patterns that earned the right to be tested again: Texas, Florida, Kentucky, Lookalike / broad delivery, Facebook Feed, Facebook Reels, and working-farrier story evidence.

Core optimization change: move the business from Link clicks management to an event ladder: Mobile app installs → Account created → Activated farrier → Paid customer → Week-4 active farrier. Mobile app installs are the temporary campaign objective; Activated farrier becomes the operating performance KPI as soon as event volume is sufficient.

Required measurement setup before scaling

1

Android App event attribution

Confirm Mobile app installs, Account created, Activated farrier, and Paid customer can be seen by campaign, state, placement, ad set, and creative. Use a deduplicated event definition.

2

iOS measurement repair

Apple App Store historical result fields are unavailable. Audit the SDK or mobile measurement partner, event mapping, and iOS attribution before allocating new Apple App Store spend.

3

Activated farrier definition

Define activation as at least two core actions within seven days, for example: horse entry, appointment, reminder, job note, invoice, team invitation, or inventory/product action.

4

Paid customer return path

Send paid subscription or verified paid status from the billing system / CRM to analytics. This makes full-funnel activation and paid-customer quality visible.

3. Expected results and budget plan

The forecast is based on historical Google Play Cost per app install. Mobile app-install volumes are arithmetic scenarios at unchanged historical cost, not a promise of future performance. The decision focus is activation quality after installation.

Important: The forecast uses a historical scenario range of €5.00, €6.85, and €9.98 Cost per app install. The 35% install-to-Activated farrier figure is a planning input, not a historical result. Replace it with product cohort data as soon as the event path is live.
